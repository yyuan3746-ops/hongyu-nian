Hongyu Nian

I am a Research Fellow at the climaTRACES Lab and the Faculty of Economics, University of Cambridge. From 2023 to 2025, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I work in environmental economics, studying topics including environmental policy design, climate change, biodiversity, and new energy vehicles. My research uses economic theory and empirical methods to understand how environmental and technological changes affect firms, households, human health, and social welfare.
